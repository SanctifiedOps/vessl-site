FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Before you 'get onboard' VESSL, we want you to have all your questions fully answered! Our most frequently asked questions are below:
Get started Get started
  • Is VESSL for beginners?
    Yes. Our program is designed for everyone from beginners to athletes!
  • What equipment do I need?
    A pair of dumbbells or kettlebells is enough to start. Bodyweight options are included.
  • Can I train around an old injury?
    VESSL is mobility-first with scalable options. Always follow your clinician's advice.
  • How long are sessions?
    We have a flexible approach. You will find that most of our strength and conditioning sessions are 30-45 minutes. We do also have sessions as short as 5 - 10 minutes in Pilates or stretch sessions.
  • Can I cancel anytime?
    Yes, you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice.
  • How do I get access?
    Sign up today using the book now link and join our VESSL community today!
